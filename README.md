# Jharkhand Engineers Hub (JEHUB) - Web App

Welcome to the official repository of the **Jharkhand Engineers Hub (JEHUB)** Web App! This app is designed to provide Jharkhand Polytechnic and engineering students with essential academic resources, study materials, and a platform to connect with their peers.

## App Features

### 1. Home Dashboard
The central hub of the app that provides easy access to the following sections:
- **Notes**: View and download study notes organized by topics and subjects.
- **Books**: Access essential books and e-books related to various subjects.
- **Previous Year Questions (PYQs)**: Practice with past exam papers to prepare effectively.
- **Important Questions**: Get a list of must-know questions for exam preparation.

### 2. Community Access
Join our community on:
- [Telegram Group](https://t.me/JharkhandEnginnersHub) – Stay updated and discuss with peers.
- [WhatsApp Group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/DuHyRtYW9nqGthezXam9yZ) – Engage in discussions and receive important updates.

### 3. User-Friendly Interface
The app features a clean and intuitive UI, making it easy for students to find and access the resources they need. Each section is color-coded and labeled clearly for ease of navigation.

### 4. Real-time Notifications
Stay informed with important updates through the built-in notification feature.

### 5. Profile Section
Users can personalize their profile and manage their interactions with the app.
